Questions

Everything you'd want to ask.

Is there a contract or cancellation fee?
No contracts and no cancellation fees. You can pause, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time from your member dashboard.
What areas do you serve?
All of Northeast Pennsylvania — Lackawanna, Luzerne, Wayne, Pike, Susquehanna, Wyoming, and Monroe counties. If you're outside our zone, we can usually still help on the Estate plan with a small travel surcharge.
What if my repair takes longer than my monthly hours?
Anything beyond your included hours is billed at your member discount (10–25% off our standard rates depending on plan). You'll always get a written estimate before we start.
Are emergencies really 24/7?
Yes — Premier and Estate members reach a real on-call tech 24/7. Pro members get a guaranteed 24-hour response window. We prioritize active leaks, no-heat, no-power, and lock-outs ahead of all non-member work.
How do payments work?
All subscriptions are billed securely via Stripe. We accept Apple Pay, Google Pay, and every major card. You'll receive an itemized invoice each month and can manage your card from the dashboard.
Do you handle short-term rentals (Airbnb / VRBO)?
Yes — STRs are one of our specialties. Premier and Estate plans include guest-turn coordination and emergency response designed for hosts.
