Property & inspection details

Inspection scope: This is a non-invasive, visual inspection of the readily accessible installed systems and components of the home as they exist at the time of the inspection. Aligned with ASHI and InterNACHI Standards of Practice.

Limitations: Areas not accessible (covered by stored items, finished walls, locked rooms, etc.), latent or concealed defects, and conditions that may change between the inspection and possession are excluded. For binding evaluations, engage licensed specialists (electricians, plumbers, structural engineers, etc.).
